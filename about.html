About Harish Silk & Sarees

47 Years of Trust

Our Story

Three Generations of Fine Weaving

Founded in 1978, Harish Silk & Sarees began as a single handloom counter and has grown into a full showroom trusted across the city. We work directly with weaver co-operatives in Kanchipuram, Coimbatore and beyond, so every saree, shirt and metre of fabric that reaches you carries a story of craft.

Our tagline, "Tradition Meets Elegance," is a promise we carry into every collection we curate — from bridal lehengas to everyday cottons.

Meet the Family Behind the Counter
